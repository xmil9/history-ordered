# Ordered

Ordered is a phone game about putting history back in order. Choose a timeline, arrange a shuffled list of real events, and check how many landed in the right place.

Timelines on the shelf:

- Battles of Alexander the Great, one round of eight engagements
- Scientific discoveries of the 18th century, two rounds of eight
- US states joining the Union, all fifty states in seven rounds
- The wives of Henry VIII, six queens in one round
- The Wars of the Roses, ten events in one round
- The Roman Empire, twenty events in two rounds
- Stops in the Odyssey, twelve landfalls in two rounds
- Space exploration, twenty events in two rounds
- Egyptian history, twenty events in two rounds
- The five mass extinctions, one round
- Advances in electricity, sixteen events in two rounds
- Super Bowl winning quarterbacks, all sixty, in six rounds of ten
- Baseball, sixteen events in two rounds
- Basketball, sixteen events in two rounds
- Soccer, sixteen events in two rounds

Dates stay hidden until you check. A best run is saved on the device after every round in a timeline has been checked. The latest check of each round is the one that counts. There is no account and no network.

Hold a card and drag it, or use the arrows beside it. On the web preview, the arrows are the sure way to reorder a row.

## Run it locally

```bash
npm install
npx expo start
```

Open the project in Expo Go, a simulator, or the web preview from the dev server.
